Roasted in small batches, on Tuesdays only

Coffee is at its best in the first few weeks after roasting. So we roast once a week, pack the same day and ship straight away. No warehouse shelf in between.

  • Every bag carries the roast date, not a vague best-before.
  • Roasts are profiled by hand and tasted blind before they go on sale.
  • Farmers get paid above the fair-trade floor, and we publish the price.
